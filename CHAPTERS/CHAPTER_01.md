# CHAPTER 01 — COLD OPEN

## Purpose
Hook the audience before explaining the protagonist's backstory.

## Opening beats

### Beat 1 — The auction begins
A prestigious auction room is already active. Jewelry, paintings and antiques establish the setting.

### Beat 2 — Lot 47
The auctioneer presents a small, completely ordinary-looking wooden box.

### Beat 3 — Opening bid
The auctioneer asks for the opening bid. The established auction progression begins at **$500**.

### Beat 4 — Escalation
The visible progression is:
- $500
- $1,000
- $5,000

The early bidding should feel normal.

### Beat 5 — The impossible bid
A man across the room suddenly bids **$100,000**.

This is the moment the audience should understand that something about Lot 47 is not ordinary, even though the box itself looks ordinary.

### Beat 6 — Daniel reacts
Daniel has no idea why the stranger would pay that much. Daniel bids again.

### Beat 7 — The stranger follows
The stranger follows Daniel's bid. The competition becomes personal and increasingly tense.

### Beat 8 — $500,000
The price reaches **$500,000**. Daniel cannot realistically compete at that level.

### Beat 9 — The phone call
Daniel's phone rings.

The number belongs to his **dead father**.

Daniel answers.

### Beat 10 — End/continuation hook
His father's voice speaks.

The exact continuation should be developed carefully rather than spoiled in production notes.
