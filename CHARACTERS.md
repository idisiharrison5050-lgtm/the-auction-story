# CHARACTERS

## Daniel Vale
- Protagonist.
- Current visual reference exists from the character/reference boards created during production.
- A clean full-body reference is preferred for body proportions and wardrobe continuity because an earlier reference did not show his legs clearly.
- His face must remain consistent across shots.
- Do not intentionally make him resemble any famous or real person.

## Arthur Vale
- Daniel's father.
- Established as an older, experienced, hands-on man associated with the workshop.
- His visual identity should remain distinct from Daniel.
- Do not intentionally make him resemble any famous or real person.

## The Bidder / Stranger
- The mysterious bidder who makes the extraordinary $100,000 bid on Lot 47.
- He should communicate power through restraint rather than stereotypical villain styling.
- He should not look like a famous or real person.
- His first major reveal should feel significant; do not waste the reveal early.

## Bidder 23
- Ordinary auction participant used for the early opening bid.
- Must have a face clearly different from Arthur and Daniel.
- Silent while bidding; the auctioneer handles the spoken bid amount.
- Paddle number: 23.
- Do not accidentally place Daniel in the foreground/background in Bidder 23 shots.

## Continuity warning
Early generated boards contained multiple people and text labels. Always crop/select the exact character reference needed for image-to-video rather than feeding a whole board when possible.
