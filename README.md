# The Auction

A chapter-based cinematic AI film series.

## Purpose
This repository is the durable story and production bible for the series. It records established story facts, character continuity, visual references, shot decisions, and image-to-video workflow so future chapters stay consistent.

## Current production target
- Format: short serialized chapters rather than one full movie generated at once.
- Current practical chapter length: about 4 minutes.
- Chapter 1 begins with a cold open at an auction and deliberately withholds the backstory.
- The story should be interesting to watch first; genre is secondary.
- Characters must be original-looking and must not intentionally resemble famous or real people.

## Current status
Chapter 1 production is underway. The opening auction shots are being built one shot at a time.
